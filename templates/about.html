About Us

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Welcome to Yatra AI (offbeatyatra.online)!

Yatra AI is an educational travel planner designed specifically to address the mathematical and spatial gaps of offbeat travel in India. While traditional aggregators focus on commercial hubs, our platform guides travelers to pristine, sustainable destinations, directing economic benefits directly to local remote homestay operators.

Core Features

  • Geodesic Transit Costing: Integrates the Haversine formula to compute distance-based travel tariffs from a user's home city to starting travel hubs.
  • Capacity-based Allocation: Calculates exact room requirements based on double-occupancy and private vehicles by seating limits to prevent budget shortfalls.
  • Gender Safety Advisories: Dynamically adjusts daily schedules and issues curfew warning parameters if solo female travelers are planning trips.
  • Self-Growing Directory: Allows users to query any custom village in India and updates our local database coordinates using LLM validation.
